Why SetupLap

Built for the rest of us.

SetupLap is being designed around racers who want a stable, useful setup without needing esports-level driving or another subscription.

The idea

Most setup services naturally concentrate on the biggest series. SetupLap aims to complement that world with free setups, stronger coverage of neglected open-setup series, and clear information about what each setup is actually for.

How setups will be presented

Every final setup page is intended to show simulator, series/class, car, circuit, race or qualifying purpose, conditions, relevant game build/season, revision history and practical engineer notes. Older setups can be flagged when physics or balance changes may make them stale.

How the site makes money

The core setup library is intended to remain free. Revenue can come from restrained display advertising, relevant affiliate partnerships and future sponsorships rather than locking setup downloads behind a paywall.

Independence

SetupLap is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by iRacing, Motorsport Games, Studio 397, car manufacturers, circuits or other setup providers.
