Why SetupLap

Built for racers, not aliens.

SetupLap exists for sim racers who want a car that feels predictable and confidence-inspiring without needing esports-level technique or another subscription.

The idea

Most setup shops naturally concentrate on the biggest series and fastest drivers. SetupLap takes a different angle: free setups, practical race balance and stronger coverage of open-setup series that can be overlooked elsewhere.

What a SetupLap setup should feel like

The target is stable, repeatable pace. We would rather give away a setup that lets an average racer confidently attack twenty laps than one that is spectacular for a single qualifying lap and nervous everywhere else.

Clear information

Published setup pages are designed to show the simulator, car, circuit, series or class, race or qualifying purpose, conditions, game build or season, revision history and practical driving notes. When an update may have made an older file stale, it should be obvious.

Free core library

The core setup files are intended to remain free. If SetupLap generates revenue, the plan is to do it through restrained advertising, relevant partnerships and future companion tools rather than putting the setup downloads behind a subscription.

Independent

SetupLap is an independent sim-racing project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by iRacing, Studio 397, Motorsport Games, vehicle manufacturers, circuits or other setup providers.
